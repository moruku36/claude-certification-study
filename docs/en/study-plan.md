# Four-week study plan

Unofficial, independently authored study materials. Not endorsed by or affiliated with Anthropic or Pearson VUE. No pass guarantee. Checked: 2026-10-13

Our suggested plan: five days per week, 45–60 minutes a day; extend it based on experience. These hours do not guarantee exam readiness.

| Week | Work | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eligibility and official guide; M01–M04, L01–L04 | Digest prompt, rubric, model comparison, source register |
| 2 | M05–M08, L05–L08 | Loop diagram, recovery table, tool contract, repo conventions |
| 3 | M09–M10, L09–L12 | Validator, risk register, architecture decision, handoff |
| 4 | Review target mapping, answer questions closed book, explain errors | Domain gap list and follow-up study plan |

- Associate: emphasize M01/M02/M03/M04/M09/M10 and explain workflow adoption and escalation.
- Architect Foundations: emphasize M05/M07/M08/M09; explain and implement each detailed guide task.
- Developer: prioritize highest-weight integration (M06), stop reasons, tool traces, and recovery behavior.
- Architect Professional: connect design, integration, evaluation, governance, and operations through M10; defend decisions to stakeholders.

Daily: read 10 min → exercise 25 min → verify evidence 10 min → log gaps 5 min. Explain English terms in Japanese, then write a short English rationale.
Readiness: explain a decision, evidence, and failure case in every domain. Correct answers to our questions cannot predict an official pass. Live API/Claude Code practice is outside this free offline pack. Add it only in an existing authorized free environment after personally checking cost and data conditions.
