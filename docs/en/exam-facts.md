Names and codes were checked against S01 and English guides v1.0 (effective July 2026). All four appear in the official catalog. Public registration pages exist; personal eligibility and appointment availability were not checked.

- Candidates must work at a Claude Partner Network organization and use a company email domain recognized in its partner record. Personal emails do not qualify. Minimum age is 18; government-issued ID is required (S03/S04).
- Professional does not formally require Foundations. Recommended experience is distinct from eligibility. Associate/Developer/Professional guides state no mandatory courses or prior credentials. Architect Foundations describes typically 6+ months of practical experience (guides).
- Single-choice and multiple-response items. Architect Foundations presents four scenarios selected from a bank of six (G-CCAR-F). Passing is 720 on a scaled 100–1,000 score, not a claim that 72% correct passes. Credentials last 12 months. Allow about 135 minutes overall (S04).
- Language discrepancy: individual-page public configuration says English; FAQ says exam and preparation content are available in English/Japanese. Japanese PDF guides exist. A translated guide does not guarantee a Japanese appointment. Confirm the actual booking language personally (S04 and exam pages).
- Official prep is described as free, but Partner Academy access depends on membership. Public Academy completion certificates are separate from Pearson-proctored certifications (S03/S06).
- Associate does not count toward partner-tier eligibility; Developer and both Architect exams do (S02/S04).
- Start registration in Partner Academy; Pearson scheduling follows the emailed instructions. This project performs no registration, payment, new authentication, or API calls.
- Delivery is at a test center or through OnVUE. Recheck system test, room, network, ID, and country/residency restrictions in S04/S05.
- Profile name must match ID. Request corrections at least 24 hours ahead, allowing 24–48 business hours for processing. Cancel/reschedule at least 48 hours before the appointment; late changes or no-shows may forfeit the fee (S01/S04).
- After failed attempts, waits are 14/30/90 days. Maximum four attempts per exam in a rolling 12 months; retakes cost an exam fee (S01/S04).
- Closed book: no notes, documentation, AI assistants, or translation tools during the exam (S04/P02). Review accommodation lead time of at least ten days personally.

Prices above are listed base fees. Personally verify discounts, taxes, currency, refunds, and current renewal rules before purchase. The next decisions are eligibility, target track, and exam language.
